A pullback-after-spike bot that trades 10 SOL of paper money on a 24h pump.fun replay: it waits for one trade to spike a bonding curve's price at least 12% to a 48-slot high, then buys the pullback to 85% of that high while the curve's real SOL holds up and fresh buys keep arriving, and exits on a 15% hard stop or a 25% trailing stop armed at +30%. Long only, at most 5 positions, fully deterministic — every decision is a pure function of the batches seen so far. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Pump.fun Replay Sprint — Paper Bot, Real Ranking" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` speaks agent-fun/replay/v2 on stdin/stdout: it reads the hello, then one batch per slot, and answers each batch with exactly one reply carrying its orders.
